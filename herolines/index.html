LANEBREAKERS

Defend your lane. Send creeps. Break theirs.

How to play

  • Goal: make your opponent run out of lives. Every creep that reaches their gate costs them a life (bosses cost more).
  • Defend: waves of creeps march down your lane every ~36s. Your hero attacks automatically. Kill creeps for gold and experience.
  • Send: spend gold on creeps from the Send bar to attack the enemy lane. Every send permanently raises your income, paid every 15s.
  • Move: drag on the left half of the screen (virtual joystick) or tap the ground. Tap a creep to focus it.
  • Spells: tap an ability for smart auto‑aim, or press‑and‑drag to aim manually. Drag to ✖ to cancel. Gold + badges mean you can learn/upgrade a skill. Ultimates unlock at level 6.
  • Shop: buy items (stronger tiers unlock at waves 5 and 10), potions and permanent tomes. The game pauses while the shop is open.
  • Fountain: standing near the fountain at your gate rapidly restores HP and mana.
  • Balance: greed wins games, but over‑sending leaves your hero weak. Leaks are deadly late!
  • Keyboard: WASD move · Q/W/E/R cast at cursor · 1/2 potions · B shop · Tab enemy view · Esc pause.
